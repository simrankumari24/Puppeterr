# Code of Conduct

## 🌟 Our Commitment

Puppeterr is a community built on respect, curiosity, and collaboration.  
We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment‑free environment for everyone.

---

## 🤝 Expected Behavior

All contributors should:

- Be respectful and constructive  
- Use inclusive language  
- Accept feedback gracefully  
- Focus on improving the project  
- Follow security best practices  
- Avoid leaking sensitive information  
- Report vulnerabilities responsibly  

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following actions are not allowed:

- Harassment or discrimination  
- Personal attacks  
- Publishing private information  
- Introducing malicious code  
- Committing secrets or API keys  
- Ignoring security warnings  
- Sabotaging CI/CD or workflows  

---

## 🛡 Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

Contact the maintainer privately:

**Email:** `conduct@puppeterr.dev`  
**GitHub:** @simrankumari24

Reports will be handled confidentially.

---

## 🔧 Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- Warning  
- Temporary ban  
- Permanent ban  
- Rejection of contributions  
- Removal from the project  

Severity depends on the nature of the violation.

---

## ❤️ Our Values

Puppeterr thrives on:

- Creativity  
- Innovation  
- Security  
- Collaboration  
- Respect  

Thank you for helping keep our community safe and productive.
